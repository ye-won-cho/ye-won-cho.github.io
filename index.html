Hi! I am an M.S. student at the Data Mining & Information Systems Lab at Korea University, advised by Prof. Jaewoo Kang. Previously, I earned dual bachelor's degrees in Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence at Korea University.

My research focuses on LLM Reasoning, Latent-Space & Sequence Architectures, Reinforcement Learning for LLMs, and Test-Time Compute Optimization. Beyond foundational reasoning methods, I have extensive experience building Korean LLM post-training and large-scale RAG pipelines, as well as deploying sim-to-real reinforcement learning policies for robotics.
